/ How it works

Import. Stack. Export.

No account, no install, no upload queue. The whole studio is one page that does the processing on your own machine.

STEP 1

Bring an image

Drop a photo or logo, paste from your clipboard, or skip the photo and start from a generator.

  • Drag and drop
  • Ctrl+V
  • JPG · PNG · WebP
STEP 2

Stack effects

Add effects or a preset. Each layer has its own sliders, and you can reorder, hide or reset any of them.

  • Hold Space to compare
  • Undo anything
  • Search with /
STEP 3

Take it with you

Export a full-resolution PNG that keeps transparency, or switch to Motion and record a loop to video.

  • PNG with alpha
  • Video loops
  • Copy to clipboard
